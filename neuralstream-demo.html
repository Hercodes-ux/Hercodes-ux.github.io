    The honest part: v1 learned popularity, not taste

    8 / 10
    of the top-10 titles are the same for all three users — and Like Water For Chocolate is #1 for every one of them. Different people, same list. That's a bug with a clear cause:

    Why it happens: v1 trains on positives only — all 55,375 labels are 1, and the model is never shown a movie a user didn't like. With nothing to push scores down, a few item embeddings grow to dominate for everyone. The training loss tells the story: it collapses to 0.0000 by epoch 3 and stays there — the model "solved" the task without learning personalization.

    Training loss by epoch (BCE, printed to 4 decimals by the training script)

    Notice user 13: the model even hands back movies they already rated 5 stars (gold badges above) — because with positives-only training, "things this user loved" and "things everyone gets" blur together.

    v2 FIX

    Negative sampling. Train on explicit negatives too — movies rated ≤ 2 as label 0, plus sampled unrated items — so the towers must separate "loved" from "not for this user." That breaks the popularity collapse and makes each user's top 10 actually theirs. The retrieval side needs no change: exact search over the retrained embeddings works as-is.
