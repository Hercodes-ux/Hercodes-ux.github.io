ABOUT

I started in the deep end of production data — 14 months at Accenture on high-volume banking pipelines in Mainframe SQL, where a bad execution plan is measured in hours of downstream delay and an audit failure is a real consequence. That work taught me something a lot of ML work skips: correctness and latency are features, not afterthoughts.

Since then I've focused on the systems side of machine learning — retrieval at scale, agentic pipelines, and causal measurement. I care about the parts that are easy to get wrong: whether your ANN index is actually holding recall, whether your agent's SQL is valid or merely plausible, and whether the lift you're reporting is causal or just correlation wearing a convincing costume.

Currently open to Machine Learning Engineer, Data Scientist, and Data Engineer roles.
